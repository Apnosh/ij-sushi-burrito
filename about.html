Our Story

Roll for the community.

It started with a simple question: burrito or sushi — why choose? We took the craft of hand-rolled sushi, the bold flavors of a Hawaiian poké counter, and the convenience of a burrito, and rolled it all into one.

Every order is built in front of you, exactly the way you want it. No shortcuts. No mystery. Just real fish, fresh veggies, hot karaage and house sauces.

And we don't just feed our neighborhoods — we give back to them. Over 3,500 meals donated to local hospitals, shelters and frontline workers across our 13+ locations in Washington, Oregon, California & New Jersey.
